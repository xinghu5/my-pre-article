# **My Presentation**

## **Self-Introduction**
Hi everyone, my name is Li Jinxuan.

I was born in Leshan, Sichuan, but I grew up in Xinyang, Henan during my childhood.

I moved here six years ago when I was in junior high school, and I have grown up here since then.  It was hard to get used to a new place.
I'm really grateful to my grandma. She brought me up for ten years. I used to very easily complain about any little thing, and my grandma always tried to cheer me up.

Now I'm a student at Chengdu Technological University.

To be honest, I used to think I'm good at nothing, but actually I'm good at adapting to new environments and staying patient with myself.
That's pretty much about me. Thanks for listening.

**Suggested simpler expressions:**

- It was hard to get used to a new place.
- I really appreciate my grandma for raising me.
- I used to complain a lot, but she always encouraged me.

## **Chlidhood**
I grew up in a small country, but there were so many interesting things.

My family always had cats and dogs. One of the dogs I remember most is a little black and yellow dog named Huanhuan, because he could bring everyone happiness.

I just loved that dog, and he loved me too. Every time I came back from school, Huanhuan remembered me. His tail would swing, and he wanted to shake hands with me. It was hard to forget him.

I had a lot of friends. I used to spend time with them and hang out until the sky turned dark. We always had a great time.

Those days are just memories now.

## **Transportation**
Which way do you prefer - public or private?

I prefer public transportation.

I think the biggest advantage is that it's very convenient in big cities.
In Chengdu, there's Subway Line 7, which is a circle line in the subway system. It connects many stations and helps people get to anywhere they want.

There is also a very good bus system. The buses have different routes and usually arrive on time.
Public transportation is really not expensive, so visitors can have a great time without spending too much money.

So, public transportation is more convenient and finance-friendly in big cities.

**Suggested simpler expressions:**

- The biggest advantage is convenience.
- The subway and buses cover almost everywhere.
- It's cheap, so tourists can save money.

## **Change**
I couldn't speak Sichuanese at all, and my parents told me that if I find a job around here in the future, it's better to learn how to speak it.
I didn't want to speak it because no one talked to me. But a man can change. And I was really sick of talking to my roommates in a strange atmosphere.
In summer, I decided to go into the wet market, talk with vendors, and ask how much things cost. It wasn't easy at first.
I learned the price list of meats and vegetables by the end of summer. Summer ends, things change.
Now I can talk to my roommates in Sichuanese in a comfortable way. That's my change. Thanks.

**Suggested simpler expressions:**

- My parents encouraged me to learn it for future jobs.
- I felt awkward speaking with my roommates before.
- Practicing at the market really helped me improve.
